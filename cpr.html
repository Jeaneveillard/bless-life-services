How AHA certification actually works

An AHA course completion card is never issued for online study alone. Certification has two parts, and the second one cannot be skipped:

  • The coursework — either in a classroom, or online through AHA's own HeartCode program, which you buy from the AHA.
  • The hands-on skills session — in person, with an AHA instructor. This is what Andrée provides, and this is what issues your card.

Anyone selling you a card for an online quiz alone is not selling you an AHA certification. If your employer requires AHA, the skills session is mandatory.

Common questions

What CPR certification do you offer?

We teach American Heart Association Basic Life Support (BLS) courses as a certified AHA BLS Instructor. Students who complete the in-person skills session receive an official AHA BLS Provider eCard, valid for two years and accepted by hospitals, nursing programs, schools and employers nationwide.
