How the timeline works

Four to eight weeks ahead is ideal. That is the window that lets us source materials rather than settle for whatever is in stock, and coordinate with your venue over access times, ceiling fixings and what the room actually allows.

Shorter notice is often still possible — ask. What a tight timeline changes is the range of choices, not the standard of the work.
