Our Story

Built on Service,
Named for a Blessing

Bless Life Services LLC was founded by Andree Lourdes Jean Jacques on a simple idea: the milestones of a life deserve someone who shows up prepared, on time, and genuinely glad to be there.

What began with notary work grew naturally. Clients who needed documents notarized also needed someone to marry them. Couples getting married needed a room styled and favors made. Families and employers needed CPR certification they could trust. Rather than send people elsewhere, we learned to do it all — properly, and under one roof.

Today we serve individuals, families, churches, schools and businesses across Massachusetts, and hand-pour candles to order, shipped nationwide straight from this site.

Frequently Asked Questions

What do I need to bring to a notary appointment?

Bring a valid, unexpired government-issued photo ID (driver's license, state ID, or passport) and the complete, unsigned document. Do not sign before you arrive — the notary must witness the signature. If the document requires witnesses, plan to bring them or let us know in advance.

Do you travel for notary appointments?

Yes. We provide mobile notary service throughout Massachusetts — homes, hospitals, nursing facilities, offices and other locations. A travel fee applies based on distance; we quote it upfront before you book.

How far in advance should I book a wedding officiant?

Three to six months is comfortable for a full ceremony, but we regularly accommodate short-notice elopements and courthouse-style ceremonies. Note that in Massachusetts you must apply for your marriage license in person at a city or town clerk's office, and there is a three-day waiting period before the license is issued. The license is valid for 60 days.

What CPR certification do you offer?

We teach American Heart Association Basic Life Support (BLS) courses as a certified AHA BLS Instructor. Successful students receive an official AHA BLS Provider eCard, valid for two years and accepted by hospitals, nursing programs, schools and employers nationwide.

Can you train my whole team at our location?

Yes. On-site group training is available for workplaces, clinics, schools, daycares, churches and community organizations anywhere in Massachusetts. Group pricing improves with class size — contact us with your headcount and preferred dates for a quote.

How far ahead do you book event decoration?

Four to eight weeks is ideal so we can source materials and coordinate with your venue. We handle concept, setup and breakdown. A deposit reserves your date.

Do you make custom candles for weddings and events?

Absolutely. Custom scents, colors, vessels and personalized labels are available for wedding favors, baby showers, corporate gifting and wholesale. Bulk orders need roughly three to four weeks' lead time. Reach out for pricing.
