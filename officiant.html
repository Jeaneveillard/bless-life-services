Your marriage licence — read this early

We officiate the ceremony. We do not issue the licence, and no officiant can. In Massachusetts you both apply in person at a city or town clerk's office, there is a three-day waiting period before the licence is issued, and once issued it is valid for 60 days.

Plan around those three facts and the rest is straightforward. Leave them to the final week and the ceremony cannot legally go ahead — this is the thing couples most often discover too late.
